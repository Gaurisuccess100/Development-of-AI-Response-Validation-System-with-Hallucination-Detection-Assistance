BANKING LOAN ELIGIBILITY POLICY

Section 1: Personal Eligibility

Applicants must be at least 21 years old.
Applicants must provide valid identity proof.
Applicants must provide valid address proof.

Section 2: Income Requirements

Applicants must have a stable source of income.
Income verification may be required during the loan application process.

Section 3: Credit Assessment

The bank evaluates the applicant's repayment history.
Credit history is considered during loan approval.
Applicants with a poor repayment history may require additional verification.

Section 4: Loan Approval

Loan approval depends on applicant eligibility, income verification,
credit assessment and the bank's internal policies.
